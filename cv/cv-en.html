Projects

FPGA Multi-Player Reaction Time Game

2026 · github
  • A reaction time game for up to 4 players and 5 rounds, written entirely in VHDL: no processor, no software.
  • 14 VHDL modules (15 instances) around a control unit: an FSM with 11 states and a data path that counts, compares and keeps each player’s best time.
  • A random 1–5 s delay from a 16-bit LFSR, false start detection, and debounced buttons (10 ms) with short and long presses.
  • The 100 MHz clock is divided down to a 1 ms tick; times appear on 8 multiplexed 7-segment digits, converted to BCD with Double Dabble.
